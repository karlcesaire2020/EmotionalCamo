Brookdale

This is cory's hometown which is fast paced and unpredictable. People from all over the world gather here, mixing cultures,voices and emotions largely.

Sound plays a great role in this environment. Conversations can be heard from far distances, and the mood of a crowd can quickly spread over from person to person.

The weather in Brookdale is extreme, either very hot or extremely cold, with no balance in between. This intensity reflects the energy of the city itself.
